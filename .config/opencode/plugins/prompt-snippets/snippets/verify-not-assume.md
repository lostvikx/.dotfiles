Use the `karpathy-guidelines` skill.
Do not assume — verify.
Verify critical facts rather than guessing.
Ask before proceeding if an unresolved question would change the work.
Only begin once you are 100% sure of what to do.
If you catch yourself being even slightly unsure, that's a sign to check first.
